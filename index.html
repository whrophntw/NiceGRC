Agent Architecture GRC Operations Security

This is a site meant to share configurations, tools, and setups that modernize workflows that fall under the "Governance, Risk, and Compliance" umbrella. In times of yore (pre-large language model), this was a heavily manual discipline.

With the maturity of ai-agents and Model Context Protocol (MCP) tools, much of the manual work can be automated. My objective is to share tooling that will amplify one's output while considering security risks associated with these integrations.

Lastly, I'm aware I could have vibe-coded a fancier site, but this is my humble protest against information overload and copy/paste web aesthetics.
